    Experiment A

    Verify C = I·ω·ωp

    Hang a known weight, time the precession, compare with the load torque.

    • Read ω from the tachometer.
    • Time a fixed swing for ωp.
    • Compare I·ω·ωp with m·g·l.
    Experiment B

    Which way does it turn?

    Spin, load torque and precession are mutually perpendicular.

    • Press “Reverse spin” and watch the precession flip.
    • Predict first, then check.
    Experiment C

    Faster spin, slower precession

    For a fixed load, ωp is proportional to 1/ω.

    • Move the speed slider and read ωp.
    • Plot ωp against 1/ω: a straight line.
    Where it matters

    Real machines

    Ships, aircraft, two-wheelers, spacecraft, gyrocompasses and drone IMUs all rely on, or must cope with, gyroscopic effects.
